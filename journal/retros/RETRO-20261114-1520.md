## Reading

- **Rain rows built from forecasts (4 of 4 wrong side of the book):** each
  one sat .07-.21 under the mid, and all four resolved Yes. The notes show how
  I got there. I read peak hourly PoP (ABQ "28-35%", CMH "peaks 50%") as the
  chance of rain on the climate day. A Kalshi rain market pays on any
  measurable precipitation (0.01"+) across the whole local climate day. That
  is a union over many hours, so it sits above the peak hourly PoP unless the
  hours are perfectly correlated. Treating the peak hourly PoP as the daily
  probability biases the estimate down. This is an estimation error with a
  clear mechanism, not variance. The open NOLA weekend row from 12:20Z
  (.13 vs .285) has the same shape.
